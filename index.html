2026 FIFA World Cup Team Composition
Where football players play professionally, where they were born and which country they represent sometimes do not match. To me, this is a reflection of global human movement. I put together all 48 World Cup teams and players' information to illustrate the mix of background in each national team.
Mountain Rescue
I like hiking, especially in Lake District. I am grateful I never had any accident although there were some nerve-wrecking moment when I accidentally went the wrong way. Nonetheless, there are times some walkers need help, and the amazing mountain rescuers come to their rescue. So I have made a visualisation to illustrate their hardwork.
Mapping UK diversity
How diverse is the UK society? In this choropleth map with pop-up detailed census data, I showed that it is a complex story, depending on where you are.
